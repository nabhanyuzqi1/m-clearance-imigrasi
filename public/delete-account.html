Account Deletion Request

M-Clearance ISAM respects your right to control your personal data. This page explains how to request deletion of your account and associated data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

How to Request Account Deletion

Contact our support team at support@mclearanceisam.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
Include the following information:
  • Your full name
  • Your registered email address
  • Your account username (if different from email)
  • Reason for deletion (optional)
Verify your identity - Our team may ask you to confirm your identity through additional verification steps
Wait for confirmation - We will process your request within 30 days and send you confirmation once completed

What Data Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, we will permanently delete:

  • Your account profile and personal information
  • All clearance applications and associated documents
  • Uploaded files and images
  • Application history and logs
  • Notification preferences
  • Any cached data on our servers

Data Retention Notice

Some data may be retained for legal compliance purposes:

  • Audit logs: Retained for 7 years for regulatory compliance
  • Legal documents: Retained according to statutory requirements
  • Anonymous analytics: Retained indefinitely in aggregated form

Retained data will be anonymized and cannot be linked back to you personally.

Alternative Options

If you don't want to delete your entire account, you can:

  • Deactivate notifications: Turn off push notifications in app settings
  • Clear app data: Clear local app data on your device
  • Update privacy settings: Modify your data sharing preferences

Contact Information

Response Time: We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Emergency Contact: For urgent security concerns, contact us immediately.

Permintaan Penghapusan Akun

M-Clearance ISAM menghormati hak Anda untuk mengontrol data pribadi Anda. Halaman ini menjelaskan cara meminta penghapusan akun dan data terkait.

Penting: Penghapusan akun bersifat permanen dan tidak dapat dibatalkan. Semua data Anda akan dihapus secara permanen dari sistem kami.

Cara Meminta Penghapusan Akun

Hubungi tim dukungan kami di support@mclearanceisam.com dengan subject "Account Deletion Request"
Sertakan informasi berikut:
  • Nama lengkap Anda
  • Alamat email terdaftar
  • Username akun Anda (jika berbeda dari email)
  • Alasan penghapusan (opsional)
Verifikasi identitas Anda - Tim kami mungkin meminta Anda mengkonfirmasi identitas melalui langkah verifikasi tambahan
Tunggu konfirmasi - Kami akan memproses permintaan Anda dalam waktu 30 hari dan mengirim konfirmasi setelah selesai

Data Apa yang Akan Dihapus

Ketika Anda meminta penghapusan akun, kami akan menghapus secara permanen:

  • Profil akun dan informasi pribadi Anda
  • Semua aplikasi clearance dan dokumen terkait
  • File dan gambar yang diunggah
  • Riwayat aplikasi dan log
  • Preferensi notifikasi
  • Data cache apa pun di server kami

Pemberitahuan Retensi Data

Beberapa data mungkin dipertahankan untuk kepatuhan hukum:

  • Log audit: Dipertahankan selama 7 tahun untuk kepatuhan regulasi
  • Dokumen hukum: Dipertahankan sesuai persyaratan statutori
  • Analytics anonim: Dipertahankan tanpa batas waktu dalam bentuk agregat

Data yang dipertahankan akan dianonimkan dan tidak dapat dikaitkan kembali kepada Anda secara pribadi.

Opsi Alternatif

Jika Anda tidak ingin menghapus seluruh akun, Anda dapat:

  • Nonaktifkan notifikasi: Matikan notifikasi push di pengaturan aplikasi
  • Hapus data aplikasi: Hapus data aplikasi lokal di perangkat Anda
  • Perbarui pengaturan privasi: Ubah preferensi berbagi data Anda
